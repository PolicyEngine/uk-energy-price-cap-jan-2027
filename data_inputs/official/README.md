# Official figures

Official figures go here once published, one CSV per source, with the URL and the date read in the first lines. Planned files:

- `ofgem_cap_2027q1.csv`: cap levels by payment method and the Annex 9 rates by region and payment method.
- `obr_efo_oct_2026.csv`: the cap path and the costings of the October 2026 energy measures (Household Energy Payment £2.6bn in 2026-27; electricity VAT £0.7bn in 2026-27 and £0.3bn in 2027-28).
- `desnz_fuel_poverty_2026.csv`: fuel poverty baselines.
- Any government costing published with a new support measure.
